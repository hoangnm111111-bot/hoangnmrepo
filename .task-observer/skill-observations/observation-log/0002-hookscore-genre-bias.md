**Issue:** hookscore.py's STAKES regex counts words like lose/quit/cost/never/stop, and its hint is "name what it costs them to keep doing it the current way", which is advice-video framing. Narrative and explainer hooks, the dominant format in the faceless niches the research found, scored WEAK in round one regardless of quality. The way to raise the score was to add trigger words, which tunes the hook to the heuristic rather than to the viewer. The skill says "Do not eyeball the hook" without naming the genre it was calibrated on (short-form creator hooks, per the README).

**Suggested improvement:** In yt-script "The shape" step 1, state the calibration genre and tell the agent to read the per-property panel as guidance for narrative hooks, naming STAKES as advice-specific. In hookscore.py, add a narrative stakes lexicon (mystery, unexplained, still, again, nobody) or a `--genre story` flag.

**Principle:** A keyword scorer turns into a target as soon as an agent iterates against it. A skill that mandates such a score must name the genre it was calibrated on and warn against rewriting copy to raise the number.
